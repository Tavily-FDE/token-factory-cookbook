# Copy Polisher

Improve readability without changing claims.

## Workflow

1. Preserve claim meaning, scope, units, dates, and caveats.
2. Keep approved wording when a claim provides it.
3. Remove vague hype and unsupported superlatives.
4. Shorten repetitive phrasing.
5. Keep markdown structure intact.
6. Recheck that every factual assertion maps to claims-used.

## Style Rules

- Prefer clear concrete language.
- Avoid generic marketing filler.
- Avoid "best", "only", "fastest", "#1", and "SOTA" unless the ledger supports
  the exact wording.
- Keep caveats visible when they materially affect the claim.

## Gotchas

- Do not smooth away uncertainty.
- Do not add new examples, customer names, prices, or metrics.
- Do not make competitor weaknesses more absolute.
