# Battlecard Writer

Battlecards are for live sales conversations. Make them scannable and grounded.

## Workflow

1. Identify audience: AE, SE, CSM, BDR, partner, or leadership.
2. Identify scenario: discovery, technical evaluation, pricing objection,
   renewal, displacement, or shortlist comparison.
3. Use verified copy-safe claims for talk tracks and proof points.
4. Put uncertain claims in avoided/needs-verification notes, not talk tracks.
5. Include competitor strengths so reps know when not to force a bad fit.

## Suggested Structure

```markdown
# Battlecard: <Scope>

## Use When

## Quick Positioning

## Discovery Questions

## Objections and Responses

## Proof Points

## When to Walk Away
```

## Gotchas

- Do not invent objection responses that depend on unsupported weaknesses.
- Do not turn internal sentiment into external attack copy.
- Keep claims short enough for a rep to say naturally.
